⚙️ Simple Process

How HYDRAA Works

A transparent 4-step process — from filing to resolution, every step is tracked and accountable.

👤1
Register & Login
Create your citizen account and securely login to the HYDRAA portal.
📌2
Lodge Complaint
Describe the issue, select category, location and attach supporting photos or documents.
🛡️3
HYDRAA Reviews
Admin verifies and assigns the complaint to the responsible HYDRAA wing or official.
✅4
Get Resolution
Official takes field action, resolves the issue and you receive a closure notification.
📂 Complaint Types

What Can You Report?

HYDRAA handles civic, environmental and asset protection violations across Hyderabad.

🌊
Lake / Water Body Encroachment
Illegal construction within FTL or 30m buffer zones around lakes and nalas.
Report this →
🏗️
Illegal Construction
Unauthorized buildings violating GHMC or town planning regulations.
Report this →
🌳
Park / Open Space Violation
Encroachment on designated parks, playgrounds, or open layout spaces.
Report this →
🚧
Road / Footpath Obstruction
Blocking of carriageways, footpaths, or public roads by unauthorized structures.
Report this →
🌧️
Flooding & Drainage Issue
Blocked drains, waterlogging, or flood-risk due to encroachment.
Report this →
🏛️
Government Land Encroachment
Unauthorized occupation of government-owned land parcels in Hyderabad.
Report this →
📢
Illegal Advertisements
Unauthorized hoardings, banners, or flex boards on public property.
Report this →
🚨
Disaster / Emergency
Fire, collapse, or flood emergency requiring immediate HYDRAA response.
Report this →
